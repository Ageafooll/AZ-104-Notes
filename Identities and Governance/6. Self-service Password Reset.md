In Entra ID, any user can change their password if they are already signed in. But if they are not signed in, forgot their passwords, with **SSPR** users can reset their passwords without contacting IT personnel. (at least Entra ID P1 license is needed)

SSPR works in this flow: 
Renders SSPR page in the appropriate language.
User enters their username and CAPATCHA
They authenticate themselves with a method (mobile app authenticatior (notification, code), email, phone, security question)
Password is reset by entering new password.
Notification is sent to user to inform. (might be from mail or if its a administrator account all admins are notified)

You can specify the minimum number of methods that the user must set up. You might enable more than two methods and choose two of them as the minimum. (if the account has an administrator role two method policy is always applied)

In a hybrid situation you might want to write back the changes made in Entra ID to on-premises directory. For password writeback you may use **Microsoft Entra Connect** or **Cloud sync**. (cloud sync is better for disconnected forests that might happen after a merger, because connect sync requires one centralized hub that every domain controller must have a trust relation with).

You may enable **SSPR** in this section under management in Entra ID blade:

In **Properties** here are three you can use for SSPR:
- **None** - No SSPR, this is the default.
- **Selected** - Only members of a specific security group can use SSPR
- **All** - All user in the Entra organization can use SSPR.

![[Pasted image 20260731155748.png]]

Then under **Authentication methods** you choose available methods and minimum number of methods required.

Under **Registration** you may specify if users are required to register for SSPR for their next sign in or how often users are asked to confirm their authentication information.
A user can register by going to https://aka.ms/ssprsetup and selecting the **Security info** tab and **Add sign-in method**.

Under **Notifications** you can choose whether to notify users and administrators of resets.

After saving the configuration users can reset their password from https://aka.ms/sspr after registering for SSPR

You can customize your directory **branding** by adding a background and logo(favicon) to be seen in login pages and portal. You may configure this from Entra ID blade under Manage and select **Company Branding**.
