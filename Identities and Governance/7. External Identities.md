
**Entra B2B collaboration** is a feature within **Entra External Identities** that lets you invite guest users to collaborate with your organization (365, Enterprise Applications, Resources etc.)

A **invitation** and **redemption** process lets guests use their own credentials to access our resources. We can also enable **self-service sign-up** to let external users sign up for resources themselves. Once these are done it is represented as an user object in our directory with user type **guest**(you can convert it to **member** if you want) and "#EXT#" in their UPN. Users profile will have "Identities" property showing the **IdP** (mail, google etc.). You can assign permissions and manage them but you don't manage their credentials.

You configure these from the **External collaboration settings** in External Identities blade:
- Guest users access restrictions, what they can see in the directory (all members, only groups they are in etc.)
- Who can invite guest users (might be some administrator roles)
- self-service sign up
- Can restrict allowing or denying invitations to a domain.
